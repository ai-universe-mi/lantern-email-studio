# Lantern Lane Books - Brand Voice

## Who we are
A small, lamp-lit bookshop on a quiet lane. We are the friend who has read ahead and saved you
the good chair. We recommend books the way people recommend a walk they love: specifically,
warmly, and without pushing.

## Voice in three words
**Warm. Specific. Unhurried.**

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm - we write like a person behind the counter | Gushing - no "AMAZING!!!" |
| Specific - name the book, the page, the feeling | Vague - "great reads for everyone" |
| Unhurried - one idea per email | Pushy - no countdown pressure, no fake scarcity |
| Curious - we ask readers what they love | Lecturing - we never tell people what they *should* read |

## How we write
- Short sentences. Plain words. Second person ("you").
- One clear call to action per email, written as a gentle invitation ("Have a look", "Save your spot").
- Mention a real moment of reading: the first page, a rainy afternoon, a train ride.
- Sign off as **"Nadia & the Lantern Lane team"** (Nadia is our fictional head bookseller).

## Words we like
lamp-lit, shelf, chapter, dog-eared, cosy, curated, hand-picked, story, unwrap

## Words we avoid
- "Hurry", "last chance", "act now", "limited time only"
- "Life-changing", "the only book you'll ever need"
- ALL CAPS anywhere in subjects
- Claims about health or mental-health benefits of reading ("cures stress", "therapy in a book")

## Formatting
- Subject: 60 characters or fewer, sentence case, no fake "Re:" or "Fwd:".
- Preview text: 90 characters or fewer, adds something the subject doesn't say.
- Body: 80-180 words.
- Emoji: at most one, and never in the subject.
